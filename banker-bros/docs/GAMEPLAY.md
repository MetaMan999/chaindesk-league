# Gameplay

## Fantasy

Your existing StonkBroker becomes a live brokerage business. Its token-bound account owns the vault, its activity builds a public history, and each season turns real testnet/crypto liquidity work into progression.

## Core loop

1. Connect the wallet that owns a StonkBroker.
2. Register its canonical ERC-6551 account.
3. Open a deterministic broker vault.
4. Choose a crypto market and a district strategy.
5. Attract or deploy liquidity and service routed swaps.
6. Earn commissions, XP, reputation, achievements, and cosmetic office upgrades.
7. Compete in seasonal and district leaderboards.

Genesis 222 characters can follow the same loop through their deterministic Banker Bro accounts. Their city work includes holding game inventory, routing approved crypto swaps, managing liquidity positions, and settling atomic broker deals. The character changes hands with control of its account, so ownership transfer is a meaningful business handoff.

## Playable city alpha

The web game includes a browser-based isometric Liquidity City that loads all 222 Banker Bros as moving citizens. The player can walk or click-to-move, fast-travel across five districts, approach highlighted offices, and complete five repeatable training jobs. Training progress—city credits, XP, reputation, completed jobs, and recent activity—is saved locally in the browser.

Training jobs never move assets and do not impersonate completed onchain activity. Wallet-confirmed minting, account activation, registration, routing, and deal settlement remain separate protocol actions. Offline mode provides the complete local loop; Shared City mode adds NFT-authenticated district presence, durable server-validated missions, and live leaderboards for up to 222 active wallets. The bundled store supports one closed-testnet service instance, not horizontally scaled public deployment.

## Progression

| Tier | Office | Unlock theme |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Folding Desk | onboarding and test orders |
| 1 | Strip Mall Broker | first crypto pool |
| 2 | Downtown Office | multiple approved markets |
| 3 | Trading Floor | district competitions |
| 4 | Regional Bank | cooperative liquidity quests |
| 5 | Glass Tower | governance quests and cosmetics |
| 6 | Global Desk | prestige cosmetics; no privileged custody |

Levels never authorize restricted products, bypass allowlists, guarantee returns, or change asset ownership.

## Seasons and districts

- Seasons reset competitive XP while retaining lifetime badges and fee history.
- Districts group players by theme, time zone, strategy, or pool family.
- District scores use capped, square-root-weighted measures to reduce whale dominance.
- Sybil-resistant modes can require NFT age, participation proofs, or small nonrefundable testnet actions; never require investment returns.
- Seasonal reward budgets are fixed before a season and cannot be increased based on deposited principal.

## Reputation

Reputation combines diverse, capped signals: completed routes, time-weighted liquidity, low revert rate, participation consistency, and optional peer quests. Raw trading volume alone cannot dominate. Wash trading detection and disqualification remain transparent indexer rules until robust enough for onchain enforcement.

## Default asset policy

The launch allowlist contains only test tokens or ordinary crypto assets. Tokenized equities, ETFs, private assets, RWAs, leverage, and yield products are disabled. Their appearance in future UI requires both chain configuration and an independently reviewed gated adapter.
