# Tokenomics and Economy

## Launch economy: no protocol token

The recommended launch uses no fungible token. Players earn nontransferable seasonal XP, reputation, achievement badges, cosmetic office upgrades, and transparent crypto-denominated router commissions. This keeps the game useful before adding speculative incentives and avoids implying ownership, dividends, or guaranteed returns.

The optional fixed-supply Banker Bros NFT collection is digital art and an onchain game character. Its scarcity must not be marketed as an investment thesis. Ownership conveys no passive commission or treasury claim; a registered token-bound account may earn disclosed router commissions only for active services it performs.

## Value flows

- A route may charge at most 1% in the output asset; the launch target should be materially lower.
- `CommissionAccounting` splits each fee between the attributed broker and protocol treasury.
- The platform share is capped at 20% of the route fee.
- LP returns, if any, come from the external pool/position mechanics and are not guaranteed by Banker Bros.
- Seasonal rewards, if offered, use a fixed sponsor budget disclosed before the season and are not proportional solely to capital deposited.

## XP design

XP is nontransferable and has no redemption promise. Volume and liquidity inputs use square-root weighting and caps. A healthy season score combines:

- consistent participation
- distinct users served
- time-weighted approved liquidity
- route reliability and low failure rate
- district quests and cooperative goals
- anti-wash-trading adjustments

## Future token decision gate

Do not launch a token merely to fund development or inflate retention. A future governance/utility token requires demonstrated decentralized coordination need, independent economic and legal analysis, published allocation/vesting, no profit promise, no pay-to-win privileges, and a separate audit. It must never represent equity, broker-dealer access, a claim on pooled assets, or guaranteed fee yield.

## Restricted products

Tokenized stocks/RWAs, leveraged markets, lending, custody, and fiat rails are out of scope. Enabling any such module requires jurisdiction-aware access controls, asset transfer-rule compatibility, disclosures, vendor terms review, and counsel. The core defaults remain crypto/test-asset only.
